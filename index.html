Inside a Gaming PC

Ever wondered what is actually inside a gaming computer? Explore the main components and learn what each part does in simple terms.

Explore Components How It Works
01

Your PC is a Team

A computer is not just one powerful machine. It is a collection of different components that work together to run games, applications, websites, videos and much more.

01
🧠

CPU

Central Processing Unit

The CPU is often called the brain of the computer. It performs calculations, runs instructions, manages programs and handles many of the tasks your computer needs to perform.

🧩 Cores

A CPU can have multiple cores. More cores allow the processor to work on more tasks at the same time.

🔀 Threads

Threads allow CPU cores to handle multiple streams of work efficiently.

⚡ Clock Speed

Clock speed is measured in GHz and describes how quickly the CPU can process cycles.

Real-world example:

Imagine the CPU as a manager in a busy office. It receives tasks and tells different parts of the computer what needs to happen.

02
🎨

GPU

Graphics Processing Unit

The GPU is responsible for processing graphics. It is extremely important for gaming, 3D rendering, video editing and many AI workloads.

🎮 Gaming

The GPU renders frames, lighting, shadows, textures and visual effects.

🎬 Video Editing

Many video editing programs use the GPU to accelerate effects and rendering.

🤖 AI

Modern GPUs can perform huge numbers of calculations in parallel, making them useful for many AI applications.

What is VRAM?

VRAM is memory built for the GPU. It stores things such as textures, graphics data and other information the GPU needs quickly.

03
⚡

RAM

Random Access Memory

RAM is the computer's short-term working memory. Programs use RAM to temporarily store information that they are actively working with.

📦 Capacity

More RAM allows your computer to keep more active programs and data available.

🚀 Performance

Having enough RAM can make multitasking smoother.

💾 RAM vs Storage

RAM is temporary working memory. Storage keeps your files even after the computer is turned off.

04
💾

Storage

SSD • HDD • NVMe

Storage is where your operating system, games, programs, photos, videos and documents are kept.

💿 HDD

Hard Disk Drives use moving mechanical parts. They can provide lots of storage at a lower cost.

⚡ SSD

Solid State Drives have no moving parts and are much faster than traditional HDDs.

🚀 NVMe SSD

NVMe SSDs use a high-speed PCIe connection and can provide extremely fast storage performance.

The motherboard is the main circuit board of the computer. It provides connections between many of the important components.

What does wattage mean?

Cooling

Keep The System Cool

Computer components produce heat. Cooling systems remove that heat and help maintain stable performance.

❄️ CPU Cooler

Removes heat from the CPU.

🌀 Case Fans

Move air through the computer case.

🌬️ Airflow

Good airflow brings cool air in and pushes hot air out.

08
🖥️

PC Case

The Home of Your Components

The PC case protects the internal components and provides space for cooling, storage, cables and future upgrades.
